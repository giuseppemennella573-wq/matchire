FAQ

Questions vendors ask

Will my clients know they're talking to AI?

Replies go out from your own number and in your tone. You can add a line like 'Replies assisted by Matchire' to your sign-off, and you can step into any conversation at any time.

What if it quotes the wrong price?

Matchire only quotes from the packages and rules you approve. If an enquiry needs something outside your price list, it drafts a reply and waits for you instead of guessing.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

No. Use your existing WhatsApp Business number. During early access we set it up with you, step by step.

I'm a one-person business. Is this overkill?

The Solo plan is built for owner-operators. If you miss enquiries while you're working an event, that's exactly the gap Matchire fills.

How does the partner network work?

You invite vendors you already work with, or accept requests from vendors in the network. When an enquiry needs something you don't offer, Matchire can suggest your partner with their starting prices, or pass on the lead, and you earn a referral fee when it books.

Who owns my data?

Your client conversations and price lists belong to you. We use them only to run your account and never sell them. An enquiry is shared with another vendor only when you refer it to them.
