WhatsApp reply templates for event vendors in Singapore

By Giuseppe Mennella, founder of Matchire.ai · Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

Most event enquiries fall into a few patterns, so a small set of saved replies covers most of your WhatsApp messages. The six templates below cover the first reply, the quote, the follow-up, a taken date, a planner who isn't sure yet and someone asking for your full price list. Replace the words in [brackets], keep the tone friendly and short, and always state prices and deposits in S$.

How to use these templates

Copy a template, replace everything in [square brackets] and read it once before you send. Change the wording so it sounds like you. Planners can tell when a message is copied word for word from somewhere else.

A few rules that apply to all of them:

  • Keep each message short enough to read on a phone without scrolling.
  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Write prices and deposits in dollars, for example "S$[price]", never just "the usual rate".
  • Always end with a clear next step.

1. First reply: ask for the date and guest count

Use this when the enquiry doesn't tell you enough to quote. It shows you've read their message and gets the two details you need.

First reply
Hi [name], thanks for reaching out! We'd love to help with your [event type].

Could you share the date and roughly how many guests you're expecting? Then I can check availability and send you an exact quote.

2. The quote, with deposit and hold date

Send this once you know the date and one more detail. Put the price, what's included, the deposit and the hold end date in one message.

Quote
Hi [name], good news: [date] is available.

For [guest count] guests, [hours] hours, our [package] is [S$ price]. That includes [what's included].

A deposit of [S$ deposit] secures the date. I'm holding [date] for you until [hold end date].

Reply "Yes" to confirm and I'll send the payment details.

3. Follow-up after about 24 hours

If there's no reply a day after the quote, send one gentle nudge. Don't repeat the whole quote.

Follow-up
Hi [name], just checking you saw our quote for [date]. Happy to answer any questions. I'm holding the date for you until [hold end date].

4. Date taken: here are your options

Never just say you're fully booked. Give the planner something to say yes to.

Date taken
Hi [name], thanks for thinking of us! Our [package] is already booked on [date], but here are a few options:

• Our [other package] is free that day, at [S$ price].
• Our [package] is free on [nearby date] and [nearby date].
• If you'd like, I can recommend [partner name], who we trust for [category].

Let me know which works best.

5. "Not sure yet": keep the hold

"Not sure yet" isn't a no. Keep the date held, take the pressure off and tell them when the hold ends.

Not sure yet
No problem at all, [name]. I'll keep [date] held for you until [hold end date], so take your time.

If it helps, I'm happy to adjust the package. Just let me know what you're thinking.

6. Price-list fishing: ask for the date first

Some people ask for your full price list without telling you anything about their event. Be polite, don't send the whole rate card, and ask for the date first.

Price list request
Hi [name], thanks for asking! Our prices depend on the date, the number of hours and the package, so I'd rather send you an exact quote than a long list.

What's the date of your event, and roughly how many guests?

WhatsApp Business basics

If you use the WhatsApp Business app, a few built-in tools make these templates faster to use. Menu names can change between app versions, so look for them in the app's business tools.

  • Quick replies. Save each template as a quick reply with a short keyword. In a chat, type "/" and pick the reply you need, then fill in the details.
  • Labels. Tag chats by stage, for example "New enquiry", "Quoted", "Held" and "Booked". It makes it much easier to see who needs a follow-up.
  • Away message. Set one for when you're at an event or outside your working hours. Say when you'll reply, so the planner doesn't assume you've ignored them.
  • Greeting message. A short welcome for people messaging you for the first time. Ask for the date and guest count here and you'll often have both before you open the chat.
  • Business profile. Fill in your hours, website and a short description, so planners can check you're the right kind of vendor.
